Programme and Project Delivery

Leading complex, cross-functional change

Silurian PM provides independent project and programme leadership for pharmaceutical and other regulated organisations.

We manage projects spanning functions, sites, external partners, systems and markets. We establish clear plans and responsibilities, recover projects that have lost direction, and lead implementation.

  • #1

    Project planning and mobilisation

    Turning objectives into a practical plan, with clear scope, milestones, responsibilities, dependencies and decision points.

  • #2

    Governance and reporting

    Creating proportionate governance, reporting and escalation so that risks are visible, decisions are made at the right level and stakeholders have a reliable view of progress.

  • #3

    Leading delivery

    Leading cross-functional teams and coordinating work across supply chain, manufacturing, quality, regulatory, commercial, finance, technology and external partners.

  • #4

    Project recovery

    Identifying what is preventing delivery, resetting priorities and accountabilities, and creating a credible route to completion.

  • #5

    Implementation and handover

    Taking projects through readiness, cutover, go-live, stabilisation and handover while protecting operational and supply continuity.

Typical assignments

  • Business and supply chain transformation
  • Product launches and global expansion
  • Site and technology transfer
  • Operating-model and organisational change
  • Systems and process implementation
  • Post-acquisition integration
  • Programme review and recovery
  • Portfolio prioritisation and governance
  • Artwork and labelling programmes, including major rebranding, regulatory changes and safety updates

A practical delivery approach

Our approach is structured but proportionate, with clear outcomes, defined responsibilities and the controls needed to manage the work.

We provide clear reporting on progress, risks and decisions without creating unnecessary administration.
