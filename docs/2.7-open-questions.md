## Q1. The readiness sentence reads seven of fourteen, not nine

Blocks: acceptance criterion 2.7.1, and the number a client reads first.

Section 3 gives the sentence as "Nine of fourteen lines can be forecast. Five need an answer from you first." On fixture 31 those two figures do not reconcile. `expected_routing.json` v1.1 routes fourteen lines as four `model_eligible`, one `model_eligible_wide_interval`, two `intermittent_methods`, two `policy_only`, three `refused_data_quality`, one `discontinued_confirm_status` and one `insufficient_evidence`.

Seven of those are forecast eligible. Five carry a refusal and are open items. The remaining two are the `policy_only` lines, which are not eligible and carry no refusal, exactly as Story 2.2 fixed them.

Nine is fourteen minus the five open items. It counts the two `policy_only` lines as forecastable, which is the same conflation of "not eligible" with "waiting on you" that defect 5 in `planner-test-findings.md` records, and that story 2.7.4 exists to make visible. The brief reproduces the defect it is fixing, in the sentence written to fix it.

Default: the sentence reads from `portfolio.eligible_count`, so it says seven. The authority order in `CLAUDE.md` puts `expected_routing.json` above the brief for numbers, and the engine and the expectation agree.

Cost if wrong: if nine were meant as "lines not waiting on you", the sentence would need to say that instead, which is a different claim and a one-line change.

Cost if wrong: one clause is deleted. The first two figures are unaffected.

## Q9. The open items line figures sum to one hundredth less than the headline

Blocks: nothing. Recorded as an observation, not a defect, and not built.

The open items panel on fixture 31 at 1 August 2026 reads "5 lines carrying 25.39 percent of your volume are waiting on you." The five line figures beneath it read 12.85, 6.59, 4.34, 1.40 and 0.20, which sum to 25.38. After an `EXCLUDE_FROM_SCOPE` resolution the headline reads 18.80 and the four remaining lines sum to 18.79.

Both figures are arithmetically correct. The line figures are each rounded to two decimal places for display, and the portfolio figure is computed unrounded from units and rounded once at the end. A planner who adds the column therefore gets a number one hundredth below the banner.

Default: nothing changes. The computation rule stands as it is, the headline stays the unrounded figure rounded once, and no code was touched.

**Resolved by story 2.7.9, merged in pull request 63 at `871dfb1`.** The computation rule did not change and the wrong fix below was not taken. The open items panel gained a foot total rendered from the same function that produces the figure in the readiness sentence, so the two agree by construction, and one sentence in the method note says line figures are rounded to two decimal places and may not sum exactly to the total. The discrepancy is still there, because it is arithmetically correct; what is gone is a planner meeting it with nothing to explain it.

Cost if wrong: the same family as defect 1, a headline a planner cannot reconcile against the rows underneath it, at a hundredth of a percent rather than at two whole lines. Left alone, a careful reader finds a discrepancy the tool does not explain. Fixed the wrong way, by summing the rounded line figures into the headline, the banner stops matching the volume share the routing stage owns and two stages start disagreeing on one screen, which is what the cross-stage tests exist to prevent. Whoever takes 2.7.9 should read that trade before choosing.
