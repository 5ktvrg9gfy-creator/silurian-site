# Story 2.10.1 evidence: the drawer, properly this time

Band 2.10. Recorded by the build session on 9 September 2026. Nothing here is
carried across from band 2.7.

---

## 1. The shape chosen, and why

The brief allows two shapes and rules out a third variation of the affordance
on the row. **The shape built is the second one: the history comes to the row
and there is no drawer to find.**

The first shape, the row opening on click, was already what the tool did. Rows
in the quality and classification grids and the open items list have carried a
click handler, a pointer cursor, a hover outline, an Open cell and a dismissing
hint since band 2.7. Two planners still did not open one. Building it again
would be the third variation the brief forbids.

The second reason is the stronger one. The second planner did not ask for a
detail view. They asked to see **the demand history behind RTG-60403**, and
that history was in no part of the tool. The drawer carried coverage, periods,
zero share, findings, classification and routing, and no demand history at all.
A perfectly discoverable drawer would still have failed that request.

So the history is now drawn on the line itself, in every grid that lists one,
with nothing to open and nothing to be told about, and the rest of the detail
opens in the table under the row it describes.

---

## 2. What changed

- **`forecast-app/app.py`.** A new `_demand_history` helper returns the periods
  and quantities each line was assessed on, and `/api/quality` returns them
  under `history`. Every period is returned as supplied: nothing is filled,
  reordered or corrected, so the picture on the row is the history the quality
  stage read.
- **`forecast-app/static/index.html`.**
  - A `Demand history` column on all four line grids, drawn as an inline
    sparkline, visible before any interaction.
  - The full detail renders as a `line-detail` row inside the same table,
    directly under its own row, and carries a demand history chart, its scale
    and a one sentence reading of it.
  - The fixed side panel, its scrim, the Open cell, the screen reader Detail
    column and the first-use hint are removed. The affordance is not varied,
    it is gone.
  - One line is open at a time, in the grid it was opened in. Four copies of
    the detail would be four copies of the resolution form, and a form the
    reader cannot see is a form that can be submitted by accident.

## 3. What did not change

- No engine, threshold, decision or finding.
- No fixture and no `expected_*.json` file.
- No manifest field and no bundle field. The history is client data: it travels
  in the response body and is written to neither. A run reopened from a bundle
  therefore has no history to draw, and the detail says so in place rather than
  drawing an empty chart.

## 4. Test evidence

Full suite, `python -m unittest discover -s tests` from `forecast-app/`:

```

```

224 tests before this story, 228 after. New and rewritten tests:

- `tests/test_planner_copy.py`, class `LineDetailInPlaceTests`, replacing
  `DrawerDiscoverabilityTests`. Eight tests: the failed affordance is absent
  rather than varied; the history is on the row with nothing to open; the
  history reads as a sentence as well as a picture; the detail opens in the
  table under its own row; the detail carries the history it was opened for;
  one line is open at a time in one grid; every grid opens a line the same way;
  the history never reaches the manifest or the bundle.
- `tests/test_workspace_ui.py`, four drawer tests repointed at
  `lineDetailMarkup` and renamed where they named the drawer. Their assertions
  are unchanged: the detail still carries the decision, the reason, the refusal
  and exactly one action slot, and the resolution picker is still a closed list
  that shows its consequence before it is applied.

Proving the new controls can fail: reinstating `${drawerHintMarkup()}` fails
`test_the_failed_affordance_is_gone_rather_than_varied`; dropping the
`${sparkline(item.sku)}` call from one grid fails
`test_the_history_is_drawn_on_the_row_with_nothing_to_open` on the count of 4;
rendering `detailRow` without the grid argument fails
`test_one_line_is_open_at_a_time_in_one_grid`.

## 6. Limitations

- A run reopened from a confidential bundle draws no sparkline and no history
  chart, because the history is not written to the bundle. The detail states
  this where the chart would be. Putting the history in the bundle is a bundle
  schema change and a golden regeneration, which is the planning session's call
  and not this story's scope.
- The history chart is zero based, so a line whose demand sits in a narrow band
  well above zero shows bars of similar height. That is the honest picture and
  it is not rescaled to exaggerate movement.
