# Story 2.10.3 evidence: say what "not usable" applies to

Band 2.10. Recorded by the build session on 9 September 2026. Nothing here is
carried across from band 2.7.

---

## 1. The two things this story had to do

Story 2.7.1 was accepted on the count and failed on scope. The planner could
not tell whether "not usable" applied to the whole file or only to the problem
lines. The readiness sentence answers how much is ready. It does not say what
the band label is about.

The product owner set the bar on 9 September: the story has to **say what the
label applies to and explain the pill itself, not just scope it. Both, or the
story is not done.** Both are below, and each has its own test.

Story 2.10.2 left this pill deliberately unglossed rather than attach a
line-scoped definition to a portfolio-scoped label. This story picks it up.

## 2. Why the label was unreadable

The same three words, clean, caveated and not usable, band a single line and
the whole portfolio. Every definition in the glossary was written for a line
and opened "This line's history". The portfolio label had nothing of its own to
say, and no label anywhere said which of the two it was.

## 3. Half one, the label says what it applies to

- The run context pill reads **`Portfolio: not usable`** rather than
  `not usable data`.
- The printed report heading reads the same, rather than
  `not usable data quality`.
- A reopened bundle records it the same way.
- All three call one function, `portfolioBandLabel`, so they cannot say it
  three different ways.
- The line-scoped labels are marked too, because the contrast is what makes
  either of them readable: the quality grid column is **`Band, this line`**,
  the classification and routing columns are **`Quality band, this line`**, and
  the line detail opens **`This line: not usable`**.

## 4. Half two, the pill explains itself

Three definitions were added to `glossary.py` in a new `Portfolio band` group,
and each opens with the sentence the reader needs before the rest of it means
anything:

> **Portfolio not usable.** A verdict on the lines taken together, not on the
> file. Enough of your volume sits on lines that cannot support a forecast that
> the portfolio as a whole is not ready.

Clean and caveated open with the same scope sentence and then say what each
requires of the reader.

The pill renders that definition underneath itself through the 2.10.2 gloss
mechanism, so there is still exactly one copy of every definition and the
screen cannot drift from the report appendix.

## 5. The printed report carries it too

Paper cannot hover and a printed report has no glossary panel to reach for, so
the scope sentence is rendered into the quality hero above the existing scope
line rather than left to the screen.

## 6. What the pair now reads as

| Label | What it is about |
|---|---|
| `ACCEPT` | The file could be read and every line in it was assessed. It says nothing about what the assessment then found. |
| `PORTFOLIO: NOT USABLE` | A verdict on the lines taken together, not on the file. Enough of your volume sits on lines that cannot support a forecast that the portfolio as a whole is not ready. |

`tests/test_planner_copy.py`, class `BandScopeTests`: a portfolio band says so
in the label; one function labels every portfolio band on every screen; the
pill explains itself and not only its scope; every portfolio definition names
its scope before anything else; the printed report carries the scope because
paper cannot hover; a line band says it is about that line; no band label
anywhere is left without a scope.

becomes `test_the_band_pill_never_carries_the_line_definition`. It recorded a
deferral; it now records that the deferral was honoured and that the line
definition must never come back to the portfolio label.
