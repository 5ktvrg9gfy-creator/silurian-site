# Story 2.10.4 evidence: one list, two sections

Band 2.10. Recorded by the build session on 9 September 2026. Nothing here is
carried across from band 2.7.

A product owner decision made on 6 September, not a defect. Two planners
reached it independently.

---

## 1. What changed

All seven lines that are not forecast eligible now appear on one screen, under
the two headings the brief names:

- **Waiting on an answer from you.** 5 lines, 25.39 percent of volume.
- **Need a commercial decision rather than a forecast.** 2 lines, 9.04 percent
  of volume.

The two policy-only lines were previously listed nowhere. They are ineligible,
they carry no refusal, and the open items panel said so by naming their
absence. Now they are on the list, in their own section.

## 2. Why the distinction stays, said where the sections are

The brief: a data answer can move a refused line to forecastable, and no
commercial decision ever moves a policy only line, because no method will
predict it. Each section says its half:

- Waiting: **An answer from you can move one of these to forecastable.**
- Commercial: **No answer moves one of these to forecastable. No method will
  predict them, so what they need is an arrangement rather than a number.**

Story 2.7.4 stated the same rule by naming what was absent from the list. That
sentence is now false, because those lines are present, so it is gone and its
test asserts the two headings instead.

## 3. Every total belongs to a heading, and none is combined

- `Total waiting on an answer`: 25.39 percent, from
  `portfolio.open_volume_share_pct`.
- `Total needing a commercial decision`: 9.04 percent, from
  `portfolio.volume_share_by_decision_pct.policy_only`.

Both are engine-owned fields, each formatted in exactly one place. Neither is
re-derived by summing line figures already rounded for display, which is story
2.7.9's rule and now holds for both sections rather than one.

There is no combined figure, and the panel says so in words. A combined figure
would answer a question nobody asked and would sit on the same screen as the
readiness sentence saying something different.

## 4. Neither section invents an order

The waiting section takes `portfolio.open_items` in the order the routing stage
ranked them, unchanged. The commercial section places each line at
`line.rank_by_volume`, the rank the routing stage assigned, and drops the gaps.
There is no comparator in the panel, so the claim that the interface computes
no ordering is true rather than argued.

## 5. The panel heading had to change too

The panel was headed "What is waiting on you?" with the purpose line "The lines
that cannot move until you answer something." Both describe only the first
section. It now reads **"What needs a decision from you?"**, with the purpose
line "Every line the tool will not forecast, and what each one needs from you."

## 6. Three defects found by browser testing, and fixed

These were found by driving the finished panel in Chromium, not by the suite.
All three sit on the rows this story builds and each has its own test.

1. **A term inside a line row sent the reader to the glossary panel** instead of
   opening the line. Clicking the Decision cell, which is the widest thing on a
   row, navigated away. That undoes story 2.10.1 on any cell carrying a term.
   Since 2.10.2 the term already explains itself in place, so inside a row the
   jump costs the reader their place and buys nothing. Outside a row a term
   still opens the glossary, and that is tested.
2. **Enter on a control inside a row would have opened and closed the line in
   one press**, because the row handled the key and then received the click it
   produced. The row now ignores key events that did not start on the row.
3. **A term on an orange field turned orange on hover and focus**, so the
   decision label disappeared exactly when the reader pointed at it. This hits
   `decision-label ineligible` and `band-label not_usable`, the two labels that
   use the accent as a field. Section 9 of CLAUDE.md: orange is a mark or a
   field, never both at once.

## 7. What did not change

No engine, threshold, decision, finding or reason text. No fixture and no
`expected_*.json` file. No manifest field and no bundle field. The routing
payload is untouched, which is why the commercial section is assembled in the
interface from fields the routing stage already owns rather than by adding a
`commercial_items` list to the portfolio. That would have changed the routing
payload, the run bundle and its golden hash, and the golden is a control.
