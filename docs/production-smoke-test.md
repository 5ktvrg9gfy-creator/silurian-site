# Silurian Assay: phone check

**You need a phone, about fifteen minutes, and the web address. You do not
need to know anything about the app.**

This is a list of things to look at. Each one tells you what to do, what you
should see, and what a problem looks like. Write **pass** or **fail** beside
each. If something is unclear, write what you actually saw; a confused answer
is more useful than a guessed one.

There are no wrong answers here. The point is to find out whether the app
looks right on a real phone, which nobody has checked.

## Getting to the screen you need to look at

Most of the checks need a result on screen. The front page is not enough.

1. Open the web address.
2. **If a password box appears**, type the password and tap **Enter**. If you
   do not have it, stop here and say so.
3. Scroll down to the heading **Prioritise exceptions across multiple SKUs**.
4. Under it, tap **Download sample portfolio CSV**. Your phone will save a
   small file. On an iPhone it usually goes to Files, under Downloads.
5. Just above that link there is a box labelled **Demand history or portfolio
   CSV** with a file button. Tap it, choose **Browse** or **Files**, and pick
   the file you just downloaded. It is called `silurian-sample-portfolio.csv`.
6. Tap the orange **Assess data quality** button.
7. After a moment a dark bar appears with a row of words under it:
   **Validation, Data quality, Classification, Routing, Open items, Forecast,
   Provenance, Glossary**. Those are tabs. You can tap between them.

**If you cannot get this far, stop and say where it went wrong.** That is a
result too, and a more important one than anything below.

## Check 1. Is any text blue?

**This is the most important check on the list, and the reason it exists.**

A button or a box that has not been told what colour its text should be will
use the phone's own default, and on an iPhone that default is bright blue.
This happened once before on the main website and nobody noticed on a
computer, because on a computer the default looks almost right. The app was
fixed the same way, but **the fix has never been looked at on a phone**.

**Do this.** Scroll the whole way down the page, slowly, from the top to the
bottom. Look at the writing inside every orange button, every white box you
could type in, and every dropdown.

**You should see:** near-black or dark grey writing everywhere. Orange buttons
have almost-black writing on them.

**A problem looks like:** any writing that is blue. Bright blue, link blue,
any blue. Also any writing that looks noticeably lighter or greyer than the
rest, as if it has faded.

**Ignore:** the date box and the dropdowns may look like your phone's own
date picker and dropdown. That is deliberate. Only the colour of the writing
matters here.

## Check 2. Do the blocks look like cards?

**Do this.** Look at the blocks of content: the dark block that says
**Portfolio: caveated**, the pale block under it with three numbers, the block
of text below that.

**You should see:** every block has slightly rounded corners, and the same
gentle amount of rounding on all of them. They should look very slightly
lifted off the page, as if there is a soft shadow under them.

**A problem looks like:** one block much more rounded than the others, like a
pill or a bubble. Or hard square corners on some and rounded on others. Or a
heavy dark shadow rather than a soft one.

## Check 4. The coloured bar on the validation tab

Some blocks have a thick coloured bar along one edge. **The colour of that bar
is the answer, not decoration**: it says whether the file was fine, fine with
warnings, or rejected. If it went missing or turned grey, the app would still
read as if everything were normal.

**Do this.** Tap the **Validation** tab. Look at the top edge of the block
that appears.

**You should see:** a thick bar, roughly the thickness of a pencil line drawn
firmly, running along the top. It should be **amber or mustard**, a
brownish-orange. The sample file you uploaded produces a warning, so amber is
the correct answer.

**A problem looks like:** no bar at all. A hairline so thin you have to look
for it. A grey or black bar. A bar in the wrong place.

**Result:** ______   **Colour you actually saw:** ______

## Check 7. The page must not slide sideways

**This is the one a planner would notice first.**

**Do this.** Stay on the **Data quality** tab with the table on screen. Put a
finger somewhere that is **not** on the table, for example on the heading
**Portfolio work surface** or on the text above it, and try to drag the page
left and right.

**You should see:** nothing moves. The page does not slide sideways at all.

**Now do this.** Put a finger **on the table itself** and drag left and right.

**You should see:** the table slides sideways on its own, so you can reach the
columns off to the right. While it slides, the dark bar and the row of tabs
above it **stay exactly where they are**.

**A problem looks like:** the whole page sliding sideways, taking the dark bar
and the tabs with it, so the tabs disappear off the edge of the screen and you
have to drag back to find them.

had never been looked at on a device until this run. **It is the one result on this
list that could not be got any other way.**

### The limits of that pass, restated so nobody reads it as more

The document names these already. They are repeated beside the result so the
pass and its bounds travel together.

- **The validation bar was only seen amber.** It can be amber for a warning,
  red for a rejected file and green for a clean one. The app's own sample file
  produces amber, so **red and green are still unchecked on a phone**.
- **A third state marker was not reached at all.** The quality exception bar
  appears only on a file with a serious problem in it, and the app does not
  offer such a file to download. Checks 4 and 5 covered two of the three.
- **This is how the app looks, not what it says or works out.** The numbers,
  the wording and the decisions are covered elsewhere.
- **One phone is one phone.** A pass on an iPhone says nothing about Android.
