# Story 1.2 quality-report contract

## Boundary

The quality engine is a pure function:

`assess_quality(validation_result, quality_options) -> QualityResult`

It accepts only a successful story 1.1 `ValidationResult` and explicit quality options. It does not receive raw bytes, open files, network clients or a forecasting provider. A rejected validation result cannot enter the quality engine.

The engine does not alter the normalised rows. Findings are observations and candidates, never corrections.

## Options

- `as_of_date`: required and recorded. An explicit request value overrides the server date.
- `grain`: optional `day`, `week` or `month` override. Otherwise inferred from modal positive spacing.
- `weighting`: `volume` for story 1.2. The field is explicit so cost weighting can replace it in story 4.1.
- Threshold values used by detectors. Every effective value is copied into the report object.
- `as_of_date_source`: records whether the date was user-supplied or server-defaulted.

## Quality finding

A quality finding is separate from a 1.1 validation finding:

- `code`
- `scope`: `portfolio` or `sku`
- `sku`: present only for SKU findings
- `periods`: affected period or periods where applicable
- `metric`: supporting metric name and value
- `detail`: client-facing fact
- `implication`: what the data allows or limits
- `action`: what the planner should check or supply

There is no blocking, warning or information severity. Findings contribute to a named quality band through explicit criteria. A `not_usable` SKU also carries `resolvable`: true where information can fix the limitation, false where the series only needs more time.

## Per-SKU metrics

Each SKU record exposes:

- Coverage: first and last period, periods present, expected periods, gap count, longest gap, coverage percentage and trailing-gap periods.
- Demand shape: recorded zero periods, zero share, longest zero run, ADI, CV squared on non-zero demand, mean, median and CV.
- Materiality: total volume, volume share, descending volume rank and cumulative volume share.
- Findings and band.

Missing periods remain missing. They are never filled with zero. Recorded zeroes remain zero.

## Detector order

1. Confirm accepted 1.1 result and resolve effective `as_of_date` and grain.
2. Group immutable normalised rows by SKU and calendar period.
3. Compute structural metrics and portfolio volume.
4. Detect extract-level staleness before series-level staleness. A common old cut-off creates one portfolio finding and suppresses duplicate SKU staleness findings.
5. Detect internal gap blocks and zero-versus-missing ambiguity.
6. Detect suspect recorded zeroes using the stated zero-share and ADI conditions.
7. Detect robust outlier candidates. For histories of at least 24 periods, deseasonalise by month-of-year median first. Use modified Z with an IQR fallback when MAD is zero.
8. Detect sustained level shifts. Exclude ratios within 12 percent of a known pack factor.
9. Detect short history by calendar span and volume immateriality using agreed thresholds.
10. Assign SKU bands, then assign the portfolio band using volume-weighted criteria.
11. Sort SKU output by descending volume and build the exception list.

## Quality result

The serialisable result contains:

- `schema_version`, source dataset SHA-256 and source validation-record reference.
- Effective grain, `as_of_date`, weighting basis and thresholds.
- Headline: SKU count, covered date range and clean-volume share.
- Portfolio band and portfolio findings.
- Flagged SKU share and flagged volume share, stated together.
- Long-tail statement naming SKUs beyond 99 percent cumulative volume and their combined share.
- Per-SKU records sorted by volume descending.
- Exception list containing only caveated or not-usable SKUs.
- Method note stating that metrics are descriptive, ADI and CV squared are not classifications, and outliers are candidates rather than corrections.
- Transformation log reused from the 1.1 findings.

## Report surfaces

- JSON download of the complete quality result.
- Printable HTML rendered from the same object.
- No PDF in this story.

The UI must make validation and quality visibly separate: validation answers whether the file may be used; quality describes the accepted dataset.

## Agreed band rules

- `clean`: no findings that limit a conclusion. `VOLUME_IMMATERIAL` does not change the band.
- `caveated`: usable with a named limitation, including outliers, shifts, suspect zeros, staleness, discontinuation and short history above the not-usable floor.
- `not_usable`: span below 6 periods, coverage below 50 percent or trailing gap of at least 12 periods.
- Portfolio band: worse of the volume-weighted SKU result and any portfolio-level finding. Portfolio findings cap the result at `caveated`, not `not_usable`.

Grain inference reports modal spacing and the number of agreeing series. Disagreement raises `GRAIN_INCONSISTENT` and names the minority group.

## Acceptance order

1. Story 1.1 regression on all four fixtures.
2. Exact structural metrics within `expected_quality.json` tolerances.
3. Required and prohibited detector assertions.
4. Seasonal outlier regression.
5. One extract-level stale finding regression.
6. SKU-share and volume-share statement regression.
7. Stable JSON and volume-sorted HTML report regression.
