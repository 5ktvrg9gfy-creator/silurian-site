# Story 1.2 open questions

Status: all seven questions answered authoritatively in `tests/quality_fixtures/1.2-answers.md`. Phase 2 implements those decisions.

Q1. What are the exact rules for assigning `caveated` and `not_usable` to an individual SKU?
    Blocks: banding and headline-claim exclusions
    My recommended default: `clean` means no findings; `caveated` means the series remains usable with any named limitation; `not_usable` is limited to insufficient usable history or coverage below an agreed minimum. Outliers, level shifts, suspect zeroes, staleness and immateriality remain caveats unless they remove the evidential basis for a conclusion.
    Cost if wrong: the report could exclude usable demand or include a series that cannot support a defensible conclusion.

Q2. What thresholds define short history, stale series and discontinued series?
    Blocks: `HISTORY_TOO_SHORT`, `SERIES_STALE`, `SERIES_DISCONTINUED` and SKU banding
    My recommended default: fewer than 18 periods is short; a SKU ending 3 to 5 periods before the portfolio cut-off is stale; 6 or more periods is discontinued or dormant. A common cut-off affecting most SKUs is handled only as `EXTRACT_STALE`.
    Cost if wrong: normal lifecycle behaviour may be presented as poor data, or a genuinely obsolete history may remain in headline claims.

Q3. What threshold defines `VOLUME_IMMATERIAL`?
    Blocks: materiality finding and portfolio band
    My recommended default: below 0.1 percent of portfolio volume, with both the threshold and weighting basis recorded in the report.
    Cost if wrong: management attention may be directed toward trivial lines, or small but important products may be dismissed.

Q4. What exact gap rule distinguishes `GAP_BLOCK` from a sparse series where missing periods are an unresolved zero-versus-missing question?
    Blocks: continuity findings for fixture 20
    My recommended default: flag `GAP_BLOCK` for at least three consecutive missing periods inside an otherwise continuous series. Use `ZERO_VS_MISSING_AMBIGUOUS` for broadly sparse series where the absence pattern does not support treating gaps as a single data-loss event.
    Cost if wrong: intermittent demand may be mislabelled as a system failure, or a migration gap may be understated.

Q5. How should the portfolio band be derived from volume?
    Blocks: portfolio band and headline copy
    My recommended default: `not_usable` when not-usable SKUs carry at least 20 percent of volume; `caveated` when non-clean SKUs carry at least 5 percent; otherwise `clean` with footnotes. Always show both flagged SKU share and flagged volume share.
    Cost if wrong: an arbitrary portfolio judgement will undermine the strongest client-facing statement in the report.

Q6. What is the source and precedence for `as_of_date` outside the fixtures?
    Blocks: staleness metrics and reproducibility
    My recommended default: an explicit analysis-date field supplied by the user, defaulting to the server date. The chosen date is immutable within the run record. Fixture overrides use the same field.
    Cost if wrong: the same file can change quality band simply because it is rerun later, without a clear audit explanation.

Q7. Should a user grain override be available in the first UI or only through the API?
    Blocks: report controls and grain-defence workflow
    My recommended default: show the inferred grain in the report and provide a compact day, week or month override beside the quality action.
    Cost if wrong: unusual operating calendars may produce incorrect coverage and gap metrics with no visible correction route.
