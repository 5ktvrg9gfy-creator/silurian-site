      Portable run record

      Keep, reopen or reproduce a completed run.

      The manifest is the proof and is safe to keep anywhere. The bundle is the evidence and belongs to the client.

      A run bundle contains client data, including SKU identifiers, metrics, findings and forecast values. Treat it as confidential and do not share it like a manifest.

      Reopen a recorded run

      Reopening happens entirely in this browser. The bundle never leaves your device, and no result is recalculated or changed.

      Reproduce a recorded run

      Reproduce deliberately runs the source again and compares the new evidence with the recorded bundle.
