How the service works

Forecast first. Challenge second. Recommend third.

The forecast is a baseline, not a substitute for planning judgement. Silurian tests model performance against actual history and simple benchmarks, then overlays the operational context that a mathematical model cannot see.

  1. 01Profile the data. Check completeness, outliers, stock-outs and structural breaks.
  2. 02Generate the baseline. Run TimesFM and comparison methods over the agreed horizon.
  3. 03Back-test performance. Measure forecast error and bias against held-out actuals.
  4. 04Overlay supply logic. Add inventory, receipts, safety stock and lead-time assumptions.
  5. 05Focus management attention. Rank exceptions and identify where human review is warranted.

Test the approach against your own planning data.

A diagnostic can start with a limited historical dataset and a defined group of products, without committing to a wider systems implementation.
