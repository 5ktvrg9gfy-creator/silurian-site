What the assessment shows

  • Data quality

    Missing periods, inconsistent records and other issues that could affect the analysis.

  • Demand patterns

    How frequently demand occurs and how much quantities vary.

  • Products needing attention

    Lines with limited evidence, data issues or patterns that warrant further investigation, with a reason for each finding.

  • The portfolio picture

    How much recorded demand volume sits within each assessment outcome.

The findings describe the history supplied. They support your planners’ judgement and do not establish that a product will always, or never, be predictable.

How it works

  1. Discuss the assessment

    We agree the planning question, the products in scope and the data required.

  2. Review the history

    Assay analyses an agreed export from your existing systems. No direct system connection is needed.

  3. Review the findings together

    We discuss the results, add the business context and agree which issues need further attention.

A record you can check

Each assessment records the data, settings and method used. You can trace the findings and reproduce the analysis under the same conditions.

As new demand becomes available, a fresh assessment reflects the updated history.
